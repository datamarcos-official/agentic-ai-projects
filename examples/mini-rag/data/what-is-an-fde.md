# What does a forward deployed engineer do?

A forward deployed engineer (FDE), a role popularised by Palantir as the Forward Deployed Software Engineer, embeds with a customer team rather than only writing product code from a central office.

The work usually includes discovering the real problem on site, scoping a solution that fits the customer's systems and constraints, building and deploying it, tightening security and evaluation, and handing it over so the customer's team can run it. In AI teams, that often means shipping retrieval and agent workflows inside an existing enterprise stack, not just a demo notebook.

The job combines software engineering with customer communication. Success is a system that is in production, understood by the people who will operate it, and documented well enough to survive the engineer's departure.
